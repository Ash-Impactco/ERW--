creditable CO₂/ha = rate × yield × (1 − discount); project cost = area × (rock + spreading + MRV) + fixed audit/baseline. Defaults sit near 2026 norms: ~40 t/ha basalt, a conservative ~0.2 tCO₂/t creditable, a 20% Puro-style uncertainty discount, MRV ~€480/ha (≈€60-80/tCO₂, per UK Grantham data), a ~€30k/yr verification audit, and a verified price near €300 (roadside/unverified projects sit far lower). The roadside tiers (≈1-500 ha) map to the EuroScut corridor; 500 ha and up is aggregated dairy pasture. This is a planning tool, not a quote.
